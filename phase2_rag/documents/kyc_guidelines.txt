
KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER (KYC) GUIDELINES
Regulatory Compliance Department

INTRODUCTION
KYC is a critical regulatory requirement that helps the bank verify customer identities
and assess the risk they pose. These guidelines apply to all customer onboarding
and periodic review processes.

ACCEPTABLE IDENTITY DOCUMENTS
Primary identification (one required):
- Valid passport
- National identity card
- Drivers licence with photo

Secondary identification (one required):
- Utility bill dated within 3 months
- Bank statement from another institution
- Government issued letter with address

RISK CLASSIFICATION
Customers are classified into three risk categories:

LOW RISK
- Domestic customers with stable employment
- Transactions consistent with stated income
- No adverse media or PEP connections
- Review frequency: Every 36 months

MEDIUM RISK
- Self employed customers or cash intensive businesses
- Customers with occasional large transactions
- Some foreign connections or transactions
- Review frequency: Every 24 months

HIGH RISK
- Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and their family members
- Customers from high risk jurisdictions (FATF blacklist)
- Businesses in high risk sectors (crypto, gambling, arms)
- Customers with previous SAR filings
- Review frequency: Every 12 months

ENHANCED DUE DILIGENCE FOR HIGH RISK CUSTOMERS
High risk customers require the following additional steps:
- Senior management approval before account opening
- Source of funds documentation
- Source of wealth documentation for accounts over $500,000
- Ongoing transaction monitoring with lower alert thresholds
- Annual face to face review for ultra high net worth customers
