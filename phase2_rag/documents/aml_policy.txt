
ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING POLICY
Version 3.2 | Effective Date: January 2024

SECTION 1 - PURPOSE
This policy establishes the framework for detecting and preventing money laundering
activities within the bank. All staff must comply with these guidelines.

SECTION 2 - CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD)
Customer Due Diligence must be performed for all new customers before opening an account.
CDD involves verifying the identity of the customer using government issued documents.
Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) is required for high risk customers, PEPs and customers
from high risk jurisdictions. CDD must be reviewed every 12 months for high risk customers
and every 36 months for standard risk customers.

SECTION 3 - SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY REPORTING
Any transaction that appears unusual or suspicious must be reported to the MLRO
(Money Laundering Reporting Officer) within 24 hours of detection.
A Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) must be filed for:
- Cash transactions over $10,000
- Multiple transactions structured to avoid reporting thresholds
- Transactions with no apparent business purpose
- Customers who are reluctant to provide identification

SECTION 4 - TRANSACTION MONITORING
The bank operates an automated transaction monitoring system that flags:
- Unusual transaction patterns compared to customer profile
- Transactions to or from high risk countries
- Rapid movement of funds through accounts
- Large cash deposits followed by immediate withdrawals
All flagged transactions must be reviewed within 48 hours by the compliance team.

SECTION 5 - RECORD KEEPING
All CDD documents must be retained for a minimum of 5 years after account closure.
Transaction records must be kept for 7 years as per regulatory requirements.
SAR filings must be kept confidential and retained for 5 years.
