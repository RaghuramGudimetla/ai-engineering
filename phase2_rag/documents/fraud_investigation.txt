
FRAUD INVESTIGATION RUNBOOK
Version 2.1 | Compliance Team

CHAPTER 1 - FRAUD ALERT TRIAGE
When a fraud alert is generated by the system, the following triage process applies.
Alerts are scored from 0 to 1 where 1 indicates highest fraud probability.
Alerts scoring above 0.8 must be escalated to a senior fraud analyst immediately.
Alerts between 0.5 and 0.8 must be reviewed within 4 hours.
Alerts below 0.5 are low priority and can be reviewed within 24 hours.

CHAPTER 2 - INVESTIGATION STEPS
Step 1 - Account Review
Review the full transaction history of the flagged account for the past 90 days.
Look for changes in transaction patterns, new merchants, unusual amounts.

Step 2 - Customer Contact
Attempt to contact the customer via registered phone number within 2 hours.
If unreachable, send a secure message via online banking portal.
Document all contact attempts with timestamps.

Step 3 - Transaction Analysis
Compare flagged transactions against the customer's normal behaviour profile.
Check if the customer has travelled recently which may explain foreign transactions.
Review device and IP address used for online transactions.

Step 4 - Decision
Based on investigation findings, one of three decisions must be made:
- GENUINE: Transaction confirmed as legitimate by customer or evidence
- FRAUD: Transaction confirmed as fraudulent, card/account to be blocked immediately
- INCONCLUSIVE: Insufficient evidence, escalate to fraud manager with full report

CHAPTER 3 - CARD BLOCKING PROCEDURES
A card must be blocked immediately when fraud is confirmed.
The customer must be notified within 1 hour of card being blocked.
A replacement card must be issued within 3-5 business days.
Provisional credit must be applied to the account within 24 hours of fraud confirmation.
